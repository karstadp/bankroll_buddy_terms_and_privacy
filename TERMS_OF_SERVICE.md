## 1. What Bankroll Buddy is

Bankroll Buddy is a record-keeping and statistics tool for poker and other gambling
activity that you play elsewhere. The App lets you:

- log poker sessions and other gambling sessions, and record buy-ins, cash-outs, expenses
  and results;
- manage one or more bankrolls, transactions and staking arrangements;
- view statistics, charts and reports derived from the data you enter;
- optionally record notes about hands, venues and opponents;
- optionally create a profile and share results with other users of the App.

Bankroll Buddy stores your data on your device and, unless you turn cloud backup off,
also backs it up to our cloud service so that it survives losing or changing your device.
Section 4 explains this, and our Privacy Policy explains what we do with personal data:
https://github.com/karstadp/bankroll_buddy_terms_and_privacy/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md

**What Bankroll Buddy is not.** The App does not host, offer or facilitate gambling. It
does not provide poker games, accept wagers, or hold, transfer or settle money between
you and anyone else. Its calculators, statistics and bankroll guidance are informational
only and are **not** financial, investment, tax, legal or gambling advice. Where the App
records amounts owed between players in a home game, that is a record-keeping aid only —
any settlement happens between the players themselves, outside the App.

You are responsible for complying with the gambling and tax laws that apply where you
live and play, and for any decisions you make based on information in the App.
